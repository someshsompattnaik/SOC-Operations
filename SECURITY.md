# Security Policy

## 🛡️ Educational & Defensive Research Scope

This presentation deck and its interactive attack/defense simulators are developed strictly for **educational, academic, and defensive training purposes** for B.Tech Cybersecurity and Computer Science students.

All simulation sequences, payloads (e.g., OWASP SQLi, XSS, Kerberoasting syntax, and ransomware timeline simulations) are:
- Mocked client-side demonstrations executed in isolated sandboxes.
- Built without live exploit payloads or malicious network traffic generation.
- Designed to train future Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts, threat hunters, and digital forensics responders.

## 🚨 Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or bug within this presentation framework, please report it responsibly:

1. **Email Contact**: Reach out directly to **Somesh Som Pattnaik** via LinkedIn or email.
2. **Issue Description**: Include a clear summary of the issue, reproduction steps, and potential defensive remediation.
3. **Response Timeline**: You will receive an initial response within 48 hours.

Thank you for helping keep defensive cybersecurity education safe and rigorous!
